leasegate

Nothing on your machine holds standing power. Every process gets a lease.

The Java library that every gate is built on. Build your own gate for any credential.

What it does

  • The kernel says who is asking. A client connects over a named pipe (Windows) or a Unix domain socket (macOS) that only the current user can open, and the gate asks the operating system for the connecting process id.
  • A human sees the whole process chain before approving: the requesting program, its command line and working directory, and every ancestor up to the desktop.
  • The approval is anchored, scoped and time-boxed. A broader anchor earns a shorter approval, and the approval ends the moment its anchor process exits.
  • Nothing is permanent. Grants live in memory and expire, live connections are cut when they do, and a warning appears five minutes before.
  • A passphrase stands behind the widest requests. The industry words for the idea are just-in-time access and zero standing privileges; leasegate brings them to the personal machine.

Get it

From Maven Central, group io.github.leasegate. The modules are leasegate-transport, leasegate-core, leasegate-vault, leasegate-sshagent, leasegate-tray, leasegate-frame and leasegate-ui.

<dependency>
  <groupId>io.github.leasegate</groupId>
  <artifactId>leasegate-ui</artifactId>
  <version>0.2</version>
</dependency>
