bwgate

A Bitwarden or Vaultwarden vault that no program can open by itself, and a human who sees every request.

A leasegate gate in front of your vault. It is its own client, in plain Java: no bw executable and no session key in an environment variable.

What it does

  • The tray logs in with your master password and holds the decrypted vault key in its own memory. Nothing is written to disk.
  • A program asks for one thing: the names in a folder, one item's secrets, a change to one item, or moving one item to the trash. The approval window shows who is asking and exactly what they want.
  • Approvals are scoped by tier (list, read, write, trash), by folder and by item. Reaching the whole vault, or trashing anything, needs the master password typed again.
  • bwgate exec puts a secret in one command's environment, so a credential can be used without reaching a shell history or a transcript.
  • Items are never deleted outright, only moved to the trash, so the trash is your undo.

Get it

One file, which installs itself and keeps itself up to date. No administrator rights.

curl.exe -fLo bwgate.exe https://github.com/leasegate/bwgate/releases/latest/download/bwgate.exe
.\bwgate.exe install

64-bit Windows. Update later from the tray menu, Check for updates.
